Census: the legs circuit

The motor neurons of his six legs, pool by pool, as the dataset names them. Facts only: which neurons, which leg, which side. What each muscle does to a leg comes from papers and lives in the movement mapping, not here. Front-leg pools gate the census because the ceremony's handling is done with the front legs.

Notes

Named only in the front leg

Tarsus levator and depressor and the tergopleural promotor carry a muscle name only in the front leg. In the middle and hind legs the corresponding neurons, if traced, are among the serially named ones.

Their transmitter hardly matters here

Fly motor neurons release glutamate at the muscle. Their transmitter label matters only for the few synapses they make inside the cord.
