## 2. The dataset (facts)

- MaleCNS v1.0 (Janelia FlyEM + Cambridge + Google), released 2026-06-08, Cell paper 2026-09-03. First complete male Drosophila CNS: central brain + optic lobes + ventral nerve cord (VNC) in ONE animal. ~166k neurons, ~11.7k cell types, ~125M synapses. CC-BY 4.0.
- Headline science: 262 sex-specific + 114 dimorphic cell types (4.8% of central brain), concentrated in higher centres; sensory/motor periphery largely identical between sexes. Companion papers: visual system, **complete gustatory (taste) connectome** ("appetitive, aversive, and pheromonal pathways converge onto compact second-order hubs that broadcast to proboscis motor neurons, descending premotor neurons, locomotor-stop circuits, and neuroendocrine cells"), social behaviour circuits.
- Public bucket, NO auth: `https://storage.googleapis.com/flyem-male-cns/v1.0/connectome-data/flat-connectome/`
  - `body-annotations-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather` (14.5 MB): `bodyId, type, superclass, class, subclass, somaSide, rootSide, somaLocation, entryNerve, assignedOlHex1/2, ...`
  - `body-neurotransmitters-male-cns-v1.0.feather` (43 MB): `body, consensus_nt`
  - `connectome-weights-male-cns-v1.0-minconf-0.5.feather` (1.05 GB) or `...-traced-only.feather` (~500 MB; excludes untraced fragments; FlyBrain uses this): `body_pre, body_post, weight` (+ `type_pre, type_post`)
- Per-type web pages exist at `https://reiserlab.github.io/celltype-explorer-drosophila-male-cns/types/<TYPE>.html` (verified for MN9, DNg11, DNg12_a, IN13A001, LB1a, LB3a, BM_Taste, GNG015, GNG095). NOT found under these names: `DNg12`, `aDN1`, `aBN1` (404) → textbook names from the literature do not always match MaleCNS `type` strings; a name census against the annotation file is mandatory before designing around a circuit.
- Verified in MaleCNS: `MN9` (1 L + 1 R, ACh; proboscis rostrum protractor) receives from GNG015 (GABA, 239 syn), GNG095 (GABA, 218), GNG120, DNge062...; labellar bristle gustatory neuron types `LB1a` (5R/6L; "LB1d" in FlyWire), `LB3a` (8R/9L), etc. exist; the gustatory paper maps LB subtypes to bitter / water / low-salt / sugar / heavy-metal modalities (exact mapping must be taken from the paper's tables or rediscovered functionally). `IN13A001` (GABA) → `Ti flexor MN`, `Tergotr. MN` etc. in LegNp(T1–T3): the 13A inhibitory premotor hemilineage is present with MANC-style names.

## 3. The published model everyone builds on (facts)

Shiu et al., Nature 2024, "A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing" (code: github.com/philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model, Brian2):
- LIF with exponential synapse: `dv/dt = (g − (v − V_rest))/τ_m`, `dg/dt = −g/τ_syn`, on presynaptic spike `g += w`. `V_rest = V_reset = −52 mV`, `V_th = −45 mV`, `τ_m = 20 ms`, `τ_syn = 5 ms`, refractory `2.2 ms`, synaptic delay `1.8 ms`, `w = ±0.275 mV × synapse count` (the ONLY free parameter; chosen so 100 Hz sugar GRN input gives ~80% of max MN9 rate). dt 0.1 ms. Sensory input = Poisson spike trains onto chosen sensory neurons (10–200 Hz). **Zero basal firing, no background noise.** ACh excitatory; GABA and glutamate inhibitory; (dopamine/octopamine/serotonin treated as excitatory there, but FlyBrain/fly-escape zero them out).
- Experimentally validated on exactly two behaviours: (a) **feeding initiation**: sugar GRNs → second-order (Clavicle, Fudog, Phantom, Rattle, Usnea, Zorro, Bract, Scapula, G2N-1, FMIn, Fdg) → MN9; water pathway shares ~67% of responsive neurons with sugar; **bitter and Ir94e GRNs inhibit** MN9 (this inhibition disappears if glutamate is treated as excitatory); (b) **antennal grooming**: Johnston's-organ JO-C/E mechanosensors → aBN1/aBN2 → aDN1/aDN2 descending neurons. 91% of 164 testable predictions correct; shuffled-weight control: 1/100 shuffles activate MN9 vs 100/100 with the real connectome.
- Stated limits: no gap junctions, no graded (non-spiking) neurons, no neuromodulation/internal state, identical neurons, absolute rates not trustworthy; inhibition onto silent neurons does nothing; circuits relying on basal inhibition are poorly simulated.
- Independent reproduction ON MALECNS (tel-0s/flyverse-core): "Under Shiu et al.'s uniform-synapse rules on MaleCNS, MN9 goes 139.9 Hz → 0.8 Hz" when bitter is added; 165 labellar sugar GRNs identified; second-order Usnea/Rattle/Phantom/G2N-1 found in MaleCNS. Their behaviour scorecard: looming escape = pass; optomotor = partial (DNa02 held below threshold); hunger-modulated thresholds = gap ("dopamine/octopamine/serotonin synapses sign 0: no route"); spontaneous turning = gap; grooming = untested ("readouts exist, assays not written").

## 4. Biology relevant to the user's two themes (facts)

- **Matcha is genuinely bitter to a fly.** Caffeine is the canonical stimulus for Drosophila bitter GRNs (Gr66a/Gr93a/Gr33a). Bitter GRN activity suppresses proboscis extension; sugar drives it. In the tea ceremony a sweet (wagashi) is eaten before the bitter tea — the sweet/bitter balance is literally what the feeding circuit computes.
- Tarsal (leg) sweet taste makes a walking fly stop and extend its proboscis (gustatory paper: outputs to "locomotor-stop circuits").
- **Matcha powder = dust.** Labs elicit grooming by coating flies in dust; flies then groom in a prioritised sequence (eyes → antennae → abdomen → wings → thorax; Seeds et al. 2014, hierarchical suppression). Front-leg rubbing looks exactly like whisking with a chasen and is what a fly does to clean its legs after head sweeps.
- Anterior grooming command neurons (Guo et al. 2022): **DNg11** → front-leg rubbing only; **DNg12** → leg rubbing alternating with head sweeps; **aDN** → antennal head sweeps. In the VNC, GABAergic **13A/13B hemilineage** premotor neurons form reciprocal-inhibition motifs onto tibia flexor/extensor motor neurons (eLife 2025, "Inhibitory circuits control leg movements during Drosophila grooming"). Rhythm: **7–8 Hz**, ~140 ms flexion–extension cycle. That paper's model producing rhythm was a **rate-based** network with proprioceptive feedback, NOT a spiking LIF, and it states "It remains unclear how constitutive activity in these descending neurons generates rhythmic grooming."
- **Whisking and knitting are the same biological primitive**: rhythmic, bilaterally alternating front-leg movement. Both user themes therefore bottom out in the same (riskiest) circuit question.
- Olfactory learning (mushroom body): odor → ORN → PN → ~4k Kenyon cells → ~97 MBONs; dopamine neurons PAM (reward) / PPL1 (punishment) depress KC→MBON synapses (Aso et al. 2014).

## 5. Hard-won lessons from the two reference repos (facts, with file refs)

FlyBrain (`fly/README.md`, `fly/red.py`, `fly/piloto.py`, `fly/dopamina.py`, `fly/ataque.py`, `CLAUDE.md`):
- Full 164,506-neuron graph (typed bodies only; edges with ≥5 synapses: drops 76% of edges, keeps 72% of synaptic mass; sign ACh +1, Glu/GABA/histamine −1, DA/OA/5HT 0 → silent outputs). NumPy + SciPy CSC, **event-driven** (only columns of neurons that fired): 0.7 ms per step at ~1% activity vs 7.9 ms dense. dt 0.5 ms, τ_m 20, τ_s 5, refractory 2 ms, threshold units, weight scale 0.03, Gaussian background noise σ 2.0 (their choice). One 16.67 ms game tick = 33 LIF steps ≈ 23 ms CPU → full brain runs ~1.4× slower than real time on a laptop; heavier configurations ~240 ms/tick.
- "The regime where a network like this does something interesting is a narrow strip between switching off and convulsing." Above weight scale 0.1 recurrence drowns the signal. "With a single point measured at 0.1, this experiment would have given 25.6× and looked like a success, when it was actually on the slope of the cliff" → require a **plateau** across ≥2 parameter values, multiple seeds, and size-matched random controls, with pass criteria written in code BEFORE looking.
- What worked: short chains of spiking neurons with massive convergence (LC4+LPLC2, 311 neurons, >11k synapses → DNp01): 14–70× selectivity vs random control. What failed: DNa02 steering ("two neurons… the direct pathway is twenty times too weak"); the whole optic lobe (graded, disinhibition-based; "0 Hz in every combination"); VNC premotor rhythm in LIF (×3 gain self-ignites, ×10 convulses; "many VNC premotor neurons are graded-voltage"); their VNC **rate** model oscillates "too easily" (21% of DN types exceed the rhythm score vs 3.4% in the paper); with noise 2.0 antennal-lobe local interneurons shut olfaction completely; KCs were not sparse (49% active) until a −3.0 bias current was added; dopamine plasticity moved MBON balance but "the control kills it" — behaviour identical without plasticity; punishment "has no lever" ("it only knows how to embolden").
- The uncomfortable caveat: "today the connectome does more or less what `if looming > threshold: dodge()` would do. We did not compare it against that rule."
- Web: browser only REPLAYS recorded spikes. 164k-soma point cloud = 2.1 MB binary (`uint32 n, float32[n*3], uint8[n] group`) from `somaLocation` (NaN for 16% of neurons). Their "Neuro-Lab" sequencer and dance modes are scripted animation with random points, not simulation.
- Engine boundary: pure function `step(world, input, action)`; "no game rule outside Rust"; own PCG32; libm for bit-identical native/WASM.

fly-escape (`scripts/connectome/*`, `crates/sim/src/lif.rs`, `scripts/reference/lif_sim.py`, `specs/done/*`):
- Pipeline: `download.py` (streamed, size+md5 verified, `sources.json`), `extract.py` (seeds by type regex + top-N one-hop neighbours by synapse mass, target 70,000 neurons, seed-touching edges only → 798,715 edges), signs as above with unknown → +1, `artifact.py` writes `graph.bin` = `"FLYGRAPH" | u32 version | u32 neurons | u32 edges | u32 rowOffsets[n+1] | u32 presynapticIndices[e] | f64 weights[e]`, rows = postsynaptic, 9.86 MB; `manifest.json` with dataset, hashes, exporter hash/revision, group→bodyId lists, provenance, `synthetic:false` guard. Python deps pinned: numpy, pandas, pyarrow, scipy, tqdm.
- LIF in abstract model units (τ 20, threshold 1, dt 1 "a model unit, not a claim of a millisecond of biology", refractory 2 ticks, noise 0.015, input_scale 0.0002, baseline drive 0.05, clamp ±2), 10 neural ticks per simulated second, SplitMix64 + Box–Muller, per-fly seed. **Python oracle generates fixtures (capturing the oracle's real intermediates) that the Rust tests must match** (spikes/refractory exactly; floats 1e-12). Ablation API: silenced neurons still consume RNG samples so paired streams stay aligned. Outgoing-edge table so non-spiking sources cost zero while preserving per-target summation order bit-for-bit.
- Record-then-play in one Worker, transferred ArrayBuffers, credit back-pressure, no SharedArrayBuffer. 16 flies × 70k neurons; 600 simulated s produced in ~150–300 s on an M5 Pro.
- Taste exists only coarsely: 32 tarsal sugar GRN bodyIds + 16 `BM_Taste` bodies → 10 GNG interneurons → 4 proboscis MNs; binary food contact; **no sugar/bitter distinction; feeding is switched off in the shipped campaign**. Finding: "mean voltage tonically above the provisional gate even without taste; use emitted spikes for discrete feeding". No grooming, no learning, no thermosensation anywhere. Made-up parts are labelled as such in code and UI (e.g. exit suction "is a physical force on the body, not a signal to the brain").
- Principle (README): "**Biology → simulation → observed behavior → game design.** We build the game around the behavior we discover. We do not start with a desired strategy and force the neurons to make it work." Brain view colours "report group aggregates, never invented cell spikes". Plain-language explanations are hand-written per neuron group.
- Process worth mirroring: `specs/<feature>/` with README + slices + visualizations + assets/evidence/NN; `choices.md` decision ledger (user vs implementation decision; Gap / Reach / Provisional / Reversal / Confidence); preregistration files written before seeds are consumed; failed results "remain failed".

Other community data points: webgpu-fly runs a 139k-neuron FlyWire brain in-browser via WebGPU at only 0.25× real time on an M2 Pro (memory-bandwidth bound; hand-written multicore Rust 0.45×) → **whole-brain live in a browser is not a v1 option**; DesktopFly takes grooming *decisions* from DNg11 firing but plays a scripted grooming animation and says so; Faiku/FlyBrain show KC→MBON three-factor plasticity is implementable but behaviourally weak.

## 6. Planner assumptions standing in for the interview (each needs user confirmation later)

A1. **Outcome**: a small browser toy + a Python lab. In the toy you host a tea ceremony for ONE simulated male fly and can watch and perturb the identified circuits that decide what it does. In the lab you run the same experiments on the full connectome. Success = the user can explain, from things they saw and broke themselves, how sweet/bitter taste becomes proboscis extension in a real wiring diagram; ~10 minutes of genuinely non-scripted play; something showable to a friend.
A2. **Theme**: matcha, because its anchor step (taste → drink/reject) rests on the single best-validated computation in fly connectome modelling. Knitting is kept as a cheap late garnish: render a session's spike raster as a knit chart ("brain swatch"). Whisking/knitting *rhythm* from the 13A/13B circuit is a time-boxed research spike whose failure must not block the game; fallback = neurons decide WHETHER/WHEN to groom-whisk, animation is scripted and labelled scripted.
A3. **Candidate ceremony ↔ circuit mapping** (ordered by evidence): taste the bowl (LB/tarsal GRNs → GNG hubs → MN9; bitter suppression) [strong]; matcha-dust → grooming command (mechanosensory bristle/JO → descending grooming neurons) [plausible, names unverified in MaleCNS]; stop at the bowl (tarsal sweet → locomotor stop) [plausible]; learn to like matcha aroma (KC→MBON with PAM/PPL1) [implementable, behaviourally weak]; whisk rhythm (DNg11/DNg12 → 13A/13B → T1 leg MNs at 7–8 Hz) [unknown, likely needs a rate model]; male-only stretch: foreleg pheromone taste → courtship circuits (the thing only this dataset can show).
A4. **Non-goals**: a validated digital fly; whole-brain live in the browser; body physics (MuJoCo/flybody/NeuroMechFly); vision/optic lobe; RL or any training to make the fly "succeed"; accounts/backends/multiplayer; claims about matcha chemistry beyond caffeine→bitter and sugar→sweet; hunger/thirst derived from the connectome (neuromodulators carry no sign) — any internal-state knob is an honest hand-made "program".
A5. **Sacred contracts**: (i) the honesty split is visible in the product: every on-screen quantity is tagged *from the connectome* / *our model* / *scripted*; (ii) Biology → simulation → observed behaviour → game design: no mechanic ships on a circuit until an experiment with a control and a pre-written pass criterion has passed; failures stay recorded as failures; (iii) one owner per concept: one graph artifact format, one neuron-model definition with a Python oracle and fixture-verified browser engine; (iv) determinism: seed ⇒ identical spikes (own PRNG, fixed dt); (v) raw data never committed; provenance manifest + CC-BY attribution shipped in the app.
A6. **Review surfaces**: CLI experiments that print a table and write a self-contained HTML/PNG report; browser routes — a bench/lab route per circuit, then the tea room.
A7. **First useful playable checkpoint**: a "taste bench" page: sweet and bitter dials drive Poisson rates on real GRN populations of an in-browser LIF; a live MN9 rate meter and a proboscis that extends or not; the cascade GRN → hub interneurons → MN9 lights up; click a cell type to silence it. Preceded by the Python experiment reproducing sugar→MN9 and bitter suppression on MaleCNS with shuffled-weight and random-population controls.
A8. **Stack (recommendation, alternatives must be recorded)**: Python 3.13 (miniconda present; `uv` is NOT installed) with numpy/scipy/pandas/pyarrow/matplotlib for the lab and data prep; browser app with Vite + TypeScript; LIF engine in **TypeScript typed arrays inside a Web Worker** for v1, behind a narrow engine interface so a Rust→WASM swap stays possible (Rust 1.98 is installed, `wasm-pack` and `bun` are NOT; Node 26 + npm 11 are). Use the Shiu formulation (Poisson input, zero background noise): it is the published, validated one for taste and grooming, it avoids FlyBrain's silence/seizure band and the per-neuron Gaussian noise cost, and with zero basal firing a neuron that never spikes influences nothing — so a browser **subgraph made of the neurons that ever spike (plus a margin) across the offered stimulus × lesion envelope is exactly equivalent to the full-brain run inside that envelope**, which can be a test. 2D SVG/Canvas art with placeholders first; optional Three.js soma point cloud later.
A9. **Machine**: Apple M5, 10 cores, 24 GB RAM, 252 GB free disk, macOS (Darwin 25.6).
A10. **No backward compatibility, no migrations** (greenfield; hard cutovers).
A11. Downloading the ~0.55–1.1 GB of connectome files requires the user's explicit go-ahead in the session that does it (first slice must state file names, source and sizes and ask).
