Matcha Fly

Host a tea ceremony for one simulated male fruit fly. Every step of the ceremony is something a fly really does, run by a circuit you can watch, poke and break in the real MaleCNS wiring diagram.

A fly cannot brew tea or knit. It can taste, refuse bitterness, stop at food, clean dust off itself with a whisking motion of its front legs, and learn a smell. The ceremony is built from those.

The ceremony, mapped to circuits

Ordered by how much evidence says the circuit will work in a simple spiking model. The game is built on the first card. Everything after it is an experiment that may fail without blocking the game.

Anchor

Sweet first, then the bitter bowl

The fly tastes. Sugar makes the proboscis extend. Caffeine in matcha excites bitter taste neurons, which shut it down. You balance the wagashi sweet against the tea.

taste neurons → GNG relay hubs → MN9 (proboscis)
Strong evidenceValidated in Shiu 2024, reproduced on MaleCNS
Experiment

Sift the matcha

Matcha powder is dust. Labs dust flies to make them groom. Bristle touch sensors should recruit grooming command neurons, and the fly decides what to clean first.

head bristle mechanosensors → second-order → descending grooming neurons
PlausibleNames unverified in MaleCNS
Experiment

Arrive at the bowl

A walking fly that steps in something sweet stops and reaches down. Leg taste neurons live in the nerve cord, which only this connectome joins to the brain.

tarsal sweet neurons (VNC) → ascending → stop + MN9
PlausibleWalking itself is scripted
Research spike

Whisk (and knit)

Front-leg rubbing at 7–8 Hz looks exactly like whisking. Knitting is the same motion. Nobody has shown this rhythm emerging from the wiring in a spiking model.

DNg11 / DNg12 → 13A·13B inhibitory pairs → T1 leg motor neurons
UnknownFallback: neurons decide when, animation scripted
Late experiment

Learn the aroma

Pair the smell of matcha with sugar and the mushroom body should shift its vote toward approach. In FlyBrain this moved the numbers but not the behaviour.

odor → Kenyon cells → MBONs, gated by PAM / PPL1 dopamine
Weak so farNeeds added plasticity rule

Decisions still waiting for you

  1. How play feels. One-second trials with whole scoops and slow replay, not live dials. A taste decision is over in tens of milliseconds.
  2. Engine language. TypeScript in a Web Worker, not Rust compiled to WebAssembly as in both reference projects. It matters from slice 08.
  3. Downloads. Python packages from PyPI, then 58 MB for the census, then about 500 MB of connection weights from Janelia's public bucket. Nothing is downloaded until you say yes in that session.
